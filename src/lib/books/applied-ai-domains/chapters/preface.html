Applied AI Domains is Volume 4, merging three originally separate add-on tracks (physical AI/robotics, image models, and knowledge graphs) into one volume of domain applications built on the core two-volume series. Each part leans on a different slice of the core curriculum: physical AI on Chapters 21, 24-26, 29, 30, and 35; image models on Chapters 27-30; knowledge graphs on Chapters 27, 31, and 36.

This volume is converted last among the four, since its chapters cite specific Volume 1/2 chapter numbers as prerequisites and those numbers need to be final first.

How to Read This Book

Same chapter anatomy as the rest of the series. Because this volume spans three previously distinct domains, each part opens with a short bridging note connecting it back to the specific core chapters it assumes, rather than assuming the whole series has been read start to finish.
