Modern AI is Volume 2 of this series, picking up where Classical Data Science (Volume 1) leaves off. This volume covers MLOps, cloud ML platforms, neural networks, transformers, fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic AI patterns. This is the part of the field the industry has been moving toward fastest, and where Solutions Architects are increasingly asked to lead rather than just advise.

This book assumes Volume 1’s foundations (Chapters 1-20: math, classical ML, evaluation, and deployment basics). Chapter numbering continues from Volume 1 rather than restarting, so a cross-reference like “Chapter 14” always means the same chapter regardless of which volume you’re holding.

How to Read This Book

This volume follows the same chapter anatomy as Volume 1: Motivation, Exposition, Trade-offs and Limits, Procedures, Worked Example, AWS SageMaker Notes, History and Further Reading, How This Connects, Exercises, Mental Model. See Volume 1’s preface for the full walkthrough of each section’s purpose.

Notation and Conventions

Identical to Volume 1: formulas in standard mathematical notation, listings numbered per chapter and mirrored in the book-companion-notebooks repository, exercises tagged [W]/[A]/[E]/[R].

Prerequisites Map

Chapters in this volume assume Volume 1 in full. Within this volume, Chapters 21-27 (governance, experiment tracking, deployment, MLOps, monitoring, cloud platforms, NLP fundamentals) should be read before Chapters 28 onward (neural networks through agentic AI), and Chapters 28-29 specifically precede Chapters 30-36.
